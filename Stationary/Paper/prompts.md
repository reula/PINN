### Introduction: 

For many years there has been a mathematical theory to solve the stationary Einstein's equations in an asymtotic region of space time, namely a region outside a ball (cite). That theory sais that certain set of boundary data close enough to flat space-time and asymptotic conditions produce a unique solution which depends continually from these initial data. Very few atteps  have been made to explore this theory numerically (cite). Here we shall show that this is possible and that it opens a new window to explore these spacetimes. 
The method used also provides a simple way to explore other systems which might be of physical importance. We are reffering to systems of equations where one solves for a Riemannian metric and some other fields equations containing the Ricci tensor. Interesting cases are the creation of Minimal Energy data sets (cite), equations providing Newtonian limit expansions to higher orders (cite), find more.

We restrict our effort to the static Einstein equations, the stationary case is not more difficult regarding the approximation methods, and we will be left as a continuation of this work.
